Fresh Pull

Clear your browsing data in one click. Pick what goes once, then click the icon any time you want a fresh start.

  1. Pick how far back. Anywhere from the last 15 minutes to all time.
  2. Pick what to clear. Eight kinds of data, each with a note on what it removes and what it leaves alone.
  3. Click the icon. It clears, shows a check mark, and reloads your tab.
Download for Chrome

Version 2.1 · 49 KB zip · Desktop Chrome 120 or newer

To install, unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, then choose Load unpacked and pick the fresh-pull folder.

It asks for two permissions, one to clear and one to remember your picks. No servers, no tracking, and nothing loaded from the web.
