State v. Chauvin

George Floyd died on May 25, 2020, after Minneapolis police officers held him face down on the street for about nine and a half minutes. One of them, Derek Chauvin, was tried in state court, and the jury found him guilty of murder and manslaughter. He was sentenced to 22 and a half years. He was also sentenced in federal court for violating Floyd’s civil rights, and a state appeals court upheld the murder conviction in 2023.
