Warframe
Price Checker

Look up current market prices for Warframe items and mods.

A quick price checker for relic mission rewards.

WFPC is a desktop companion that reads your relic reward screen and tells you what everything is worth. No manual searching & no guesswork.


Define your capture region once, hit Capture when rewards appear, and WFPC does the rest. It reads the item names using OCR, matches them against every known prime item on warframe.market, and lays out the current best buy prices in card-style result panels. Individual part prices, full set prices, and which option puts the most platinum in your pocket, all visible before you have to pick.

How it works

Setup is a guided three-step flow: refresh market data, define your capture region, then jump into in-game mode. The region selector lets you draw a rectangle directly over the screen area you want to read, with multi-monitor support so you can pick exactly which display to capture from. Your region is saved to disk and remembered across sessions.


Once everything's set up, In Game mode hides the main window and drops a small floating panel onto your gaming monitor with just two buttons: Capture and Back. When rewards appear, click Capture and WFPC grabs a screenshot of your defined region, runs it through OCR, and pulls live pricing from warframe.market. Results render as responsive card panels that reflow based on window size, with the best set price and best parts total highlighted so the right pick stands out.

Built with

Tesseract OCR and OpenCV for screen reading and image processing. The warframe.market API for live pricing data. Tkinter for the GUI, with mss handling screen capture across monitors. Runs locally on Windows.


WFPC was made for the moments in Warframe where you need a price and you need it now, without leaving the game to go find it.
