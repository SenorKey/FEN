WFPCwarframe.market price check Free · open source · runs locally on Windows

Four rewards.One right pick.

WFPC reads Warframe's relic reward screen, checks every item against warframe.market, and shows you which one puts the most platinum in your pocket — before you have to choose.

▸ Download for Windows .exe installer
  • Source on GitHub ↗
  • Privacy policy

Windows builds are code-signed for free by the SignPath Foundation (certificate by SignPath Foundation).

  1. 01Refresh

    Pull current prices for every prime part from warframe.market.

  2. 02Region

    Draw a box over the reward names once. Any monitor, remembered between sessions.

  3. 03Capture

    In Game mode leaves a two-button panel on screen. Rewards appear, you hit Capture.

  4. +Built with

    Tesseract OCR and OpenCV, on your machine. Only the item names leave it, to look up prices.
