How this works

Nothing here is real money.

You get a fake $100,000 balance. You cannot deposit, withdraw, win or lose anything. There is no account, no sign-up and no email — your portfolio is stored in this browser and nowhere else.

Prices are delayed, and orders fill forward.

Free market data runs behind the real market. That would make a trading game trivially easy, so an order fills at the next price the server fetches after you submit it, never the price you were looking at when you clicked.

Hints explain; they never advise.

Later on, this page will show you what a chart looks like to different schools of thought — and where those schools flatly disagree with each other. That disagreement is the lesson. It will never tell you what to buy or sell.
